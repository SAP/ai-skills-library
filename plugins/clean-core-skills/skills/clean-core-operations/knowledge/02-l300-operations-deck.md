## Section 1: Why Does Clean Core Operations Matter?

### Slide 4 — IT Transformation Enables Business Transformation

> *"Every business is a digital business."*

SAP's vision: "We bring out the best in every business."

**Digital delivery imperatives:**
- Provide and manage state-of-the-art technology
- Support changing business models
- Embed agile innovation
- Become a business enabler and innovation driver
- Differentiate at scale with resilience
- Require innovations at the edge, standardize the core
- Reduce opportunity cost of slower feature delivery
- Manage the transformation in parallel to ongoing micro-innovations
- Leverage data
- Manage change fatigue

**Business transformation imperatives:**
- Business, process and product transparency
- Ensure TCO transparency
- Give consumers what they need
- Manage hybrid environments
- Enable the digital change

### Slide 5 — Why You Need Clean Core Operations

> *"Clean core turns the idea of 'keeping SAP as standard as possible' into a practical way to run and evolve your SAP Cloud ERP Private system. It uses five principles — Processes, Extensibility, Data, Integration, and Operations — to make agility measurable, governance clear, and change sustainable."*

> *"Operations provides the foundation: the cross-domain governance, practices, tools, and software that keep clean core consistent across transformations and sustained beyond go-live."*

### Slide 9 — Why Think About Operations?

Key reasons to embed operations thinking from day one:

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| **New operating model required** | RISE with SAP and SAP Business Suite bring significant changes to People, Processes, and Tools. Operations is the foundational principle that brings methodology, toolchain, and software stack together to support all principles. |
| **Operations enables agility** | IT needs to transform to deliver in an agile manner. Clean core operations is designed to enable agility at scale. |
| **Operations ≠ outsourcing** | The focus switches from "keeping the lights on" to providing continuous, agile innovation at scale. |
| **Operations starts before go-live** | Operations is a cross-principle topic to be considered from project initiation: governance, agile methodology, organization setup, tooling and more. |
| **Cross-team and cross-organization** | Requires significant partnership between SAP (during RISE with SAP journey), partners (SIs, AMS providers), and customer IT and business teams. |
| **Operations embeds the required governance** | The purpose of clean core is to allow continuous innovation adoption at scale. The governance (organizational, process-wise, best practices, or tool) is embedded in operations. |

## Section 3: Key Guidelines for Clean Core Operations

### Slide 15-16 — Operations Key Guidelines

1. **Leverage SAP innovations** — stay current with SAP updates to maximize business value and ensure operational resilience
2. **Embed governance and agile practices** across the application lifecycle — embed strong governance frameworks to standardize processes, minimize customizations, and enable scalable, agile operations; leverage best aspects of customers, partners, and SAP working together
3. **Embed proactive agile methods** to increase the speed of innovation delivery while balancing system stability and security
4. **Enable the integrated toolchain** to support process and solution design, implementation, monitoring, and security — eliminate silos between IT and business operations

### Slides 17-20 — Operations Is Cross-Principle

Operations acts as an enabler across other principles, in addition to aspects that determine the performance of the principle itself.

> *"Operations as a central tenet focuses on the IT transformation to support business transformation through best practice organization setup, governance, and tools. Operations embeds and enables the cloud and agile mindset within an organization that is undergoing a transformation, placing IT and business transformation as a symbiotic relationship, and facilitates clear communication pathways and strategy alignment between business and technical solutions."*

### Slides 19-20 — Introduction: Four Dimensions of Operations

```
Operations
├── Governance
├── Software stack
├── Operational practices
└── Integrated toolchain
```

## Section 4a: Embed Governance and Operational Practices

### Slide 22 — Governance and Operational Practices Core Topics

| Practice | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Customer Centre of Expertise (cCOE)** | Establish the governance and capabilities needed to support the delivery and management of the SAP Business Suite |
| **Solution Standardization Board (SSB)** | Establish the processes, people, and tools to track and execute the SSB approach. Individual principles provide specific requirements to manage deviations in their context. |
| **SAP Activate** | Adopt and accelerate your implementation with best practices and accelerators for implementation |
| **RISE with SAP Methodology** | Utilize the frameworks, guidance, and toolchain to transform to and continuously improve on SAP Business Suite |
| **Organizational Change Management (OCM)** | Ensure your end users and IT are ready to use SAP Business Suite to its full potential |
| **Operations Control Center (OCC)** | Create and maintain the transparency needed to manage operational, tactical, and strategic IT decisions |
| **Application Lifecycle Management and DevOps** | DevOps is the manifestation of agile principles, supporting the implementation and operation of the SAP Solution while continuously tracking and improving. ALM is the embodiment of the "Dev" aspect of DevOps. |
| **Release management** | Ensure you take advantage of the latest innovations from SAP |
| **RISE with SAP Methodology dashboard** | Track your progress with a comprehensive measurement framework |

### Additional Resources (from L300 slides 38-45)

- Apply the RISE with SAP Methodology to support transformation
- Set up a Customer Centre of Expertise (cCOE)
- Establish a Solution Standardization Board (SSB)
- Use SAP Activate for agile project delivery
- Empower users via Organizational Change Management
- Run an Operations Control Center as a core capability
- Embrace agile Application Lifecycle Management and DevOps approaches
- Implement a robust release management strategy

## Section 4b: Enable the Integrated Toolchain

### Slides 23-26 — The Integrated Toolchain

> *"The integrated toolchain ensures streamlined processes and enhanced collaboration across all business functions. It accelerates innovation and value realization, enabling organizations to achieve new levels of operational excellence and competitive advantage."*

Key capabilities:
- Consistent reuse of information throughout the solution lifecycle
- Alignment of strategy, architecture, process, and solution design & implementation
- Reliable solutions, proactive monitoring at both technical and process levels
- Greater end-user adoption
- Continuous innovation without compromising SAP core integrity

Operations plays a critical role in establishing the full toolchain, integrating supporting processes and organizational aspects. It also focuses on implementation and operational execution, ensuring smooth transitions and long-term success.

**Core toolchain components (Slide 25):**

| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| SAP Signavio | Managing and optimizing business processes; fostering agility |
| SAP LeanIX | Visualize, assess, and manage transitions toward target IT architecture |
| SAP Cloud ALM | Digital backbone: project management, solution operation, tailored project road maps with SAP Activate templates |
| SAP Build | Apps, process automation, digital workspaces (low-code, pro-code, generative AI) |
| WalkMe | In-app guidance and contextual learning for technology adoption |
| SAP Business Transformation Center | End-to-end data transformation process optimization |

**Extended toolchain (Slide 26) adds:**
- Digital discovery assessment
- RISE with SAP System Transition Workbench
- SAP Test Automation by Tricentis / SAP Enterprise Continuous Testing by Tricentis
- SAP Business AI (Joule for Consultants and Developers)
- SAP Solution Extensions partners

### Slide 27 — Software Stack: The Core

> *"At the core of the clean core is the SAP Business Suite with SAP S/4HANA Cloud as the central tenet."*

**Why staying current matters:**

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Innovation readiness** | Staying on a recent SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition release or an up-to-date FPS ensures access to new innovations as soon as they're available. Enables businesses to leverage cutting-edge SAP features and stay ahead of competitors. |
| **Operational risk reduction** | Up-to-date Support Package Stacks (SPS) are critical for system security. |
| **Cybersecurity risk reduction** | Both SAP and customers share a strong interest in innovation adoption and security compliance. Regular updates ensure a secure, high-performing, and future-ready SAP environment. |

## Section 4c: Performance of Effective IT Operations

### Slide 29 — Four Principle-Specific Focus Areas

1. **Operational efficiency** — embed and measure operational efficiency and excellence of SAP Business Suite
2. **Secure operations** — build your landscape in accordance with SAP's security baseline
3. **Agile delivery and operational excellence (DevOps)** — increase agility in speed and quality of innovation adoption
4. **Track and reduce technical debt** — ensure requirements are tracked and, when not clean-core compliant, considered for future innovation projects

### Slide 30 — Operational Efficiency (Detail)

- Leverage the integrated toolchain
- Utilize the RISE with SAP Methodology
- Understand key responsibilities and ownerships of customer and partner teams
- Manage technical landscape, performance, integration, and system resources
  - Note: SAP takes care of resources for SaaS solutions; customers still manage resources on custom SAP BTP apps
- Ensure quality of service to the business by extending monitoring and alerting from a business and technical perspective
- Similar approaches required across all aspects of the solution: SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, etc.

### Slide 31 — Secure Operations (Detail)

- Adopt at least the **minimum security adherence** according to the SAP security baseline
- Adopt practices with regard to the **SAP Secure Operations Map**
- Assess needs beyond the minimum baseline level in accordance with business needs

### Slide 32 — Agile Delivery and Operational Excellence (Detail)

**DevOps — build the organization, processes, and tools to:**
- Discover, analyze, design, implement, and operate your SAP Business Suite
- Increase velocity and quality of change, efficiency in deploying innovations, speed of response to issues, and reliability of SAP Business Suite
- Delivery processes include: change and deployment management; test planning, execution, and automation; increased test coverage and automation
- Increase innovation rate, frequency of deployment, and reduction of incidents; improve technical performance and data consistency
- Continuously improve the delivery of IT operations as a service to the business

### Slide 33 — Track and Reduce Technical Debt (Detail)

- Continuously manage and revisit requirements during all future transformations to standardize with new capabilities as they become available
- Establish cross-principle requirements management, in tandem with continuous innovation via the Solution Standardization Board
- Measure and track to continuously improve in line with the SAP product road map
